Our story

A brick business that chose sweetness.

"Every bite takes me back to my childhood."

Customer, Delhi

1990

The idea of Shagun Sweets takes shape when the patriarch of the Bansal family, Mr Rakesh Bansal, moves from brick manufacturing into sweets and fast food.

Focus

The family narrows its focus to sweets, and the name is chosen from the tradition of starting any good work with a shagun, the sweet offered to mark a beginning.

200+

Today the kitchen produces more than 200 varieties of delicacies, all prepared in pure desi ghee.

Now

The same promise, carried online: the taste of tradition delivered to a new generation, with the shop app and website for ordering.
